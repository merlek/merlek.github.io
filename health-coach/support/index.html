Privacy policy

Health Coach support

Updated October 23, 2026

Health Coach helps you plan and record workouts on your iPhone. Your journal is stored on your device, so preserve it before troubleshooting.

Get help or report a beta problem

For a TestFlight build, open TestFlight, select Health Coach Access and use Send Beta Feedback if it is available. Include your app version and build, iOS version, what you were doing, and what you expected to happen. Review screenshots and remove personal details before sending. You do not need to send a journal backup or private health records to describe a problem.

Email merlek.apps.support@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. We use diagnostics and messages to investigate problems and respond to you. We keep developer-controlled copies only as needed for that purpose, then delete them. Apple controls its own TestFlight records and retention.

Protect your journal

Open Settings → Data & backups → Export backup. Save the JSON file somewhere you control and check that it is available. Export Excel (.xlsx) provides a workbook for review or compatible import; keep a JSON backup before replacing or restoring your journal. A chosen cloud document provider may upload your saved file.

If data will not load or save, keep the app open when possible and use any offered recovery export. Keep the original backup unchanged. Restoring replaces your current journal, so export current data first. Avoid deleting the app or clearing workout data while investigating missing history.

Apple Health shows no readings

In Settings → Apple Health, request read access, then choose Read recent samples. Check the app’s permissions in the Health app. An empty result can mean no records in the recent window or access being disabled; completing the permission dialog does not confirm access. Step records are individual samples, not a daily total.

Timers and alerts

Review Settings → Timers & alerts and the app’s iOS notification and Live Activity permissions if alerts or Lock Screen timers are unavailable.

Remove data

Settings → Data & backups → Clear all workout data removes the local journal and recovery copy after confirmation. History’s individual Delete action hides entries and supports Undo, rather than erasing their original records. Apple Health records and exports or backups saved elsewhere need to be managed separately. See the privacy policy for details.

Beta availability

TestFlight builds are temporary and may expire. Use TestFlight to check for an available update. Preserve your journal backup before changing installations.
