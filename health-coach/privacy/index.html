Health Coach privacy policy

Updated October 23, 2026

Health Coach is an iPhone workout journal. Your journal and workout calculations run on your device. The app does not require a Health Coach account and has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics service or developer-operated journal server.

Your journal

The app stores the workout plans, sets, results, notes, schedules and preferences you enter, along with records and import information from files you choose. Optional calculator inputs are also stored locally. This information supports your journal, workout planning, progress views and calculations. A local recovery copy may be retained when replacing or restoring a journal.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. With your permission, the app reads recent step counts, body weight and workout durations to display them on your device. Records can include their dates, identifiers and source app names. These readings stay in the app’s memory; they are not saved into your journal or sent to the developer. Health Coach does not write to Apple Health on your iPhone or read it in the background.

You can change or revoke access in the Health app under your profile’s Privacy → Apps entry for Health Coach Access. Permission labels can vary by iOS version. Revoking access stops future permitted reads; it does not delete Apple Health records. Quit the app to discard its in-memory display of readings. See Apple’s Health data controls.

Files, backups and sharing

Imports are processed on your device. When you export a JSON backup or Excel workbook, you choose where to save or share it using iOS. Your selected Files provider, cloud storage service or recipient may receive that file under its own privacy practices. Journal files may also be included in iOS, iCloud or computer backups according to your settings. See Apple’s backup information. The developer does not automatically receive these files. Backups and exports can contain sensitive workout details and original imported records, including records hidden from History.

Retention and deletion

Your journal remains on the device until you clear or replace it. Deleting an individual workout from History hides its records and supports Undo; it does not erase those records from the underlying journal or exports.

To clear the local journal, open Settings → Data & backups → Clear all workout data and confirm. This clears workout records, imported history, plans, calculator inputs, schedules, timers and the local recovery copy. The app also attempts to remove its temporary export and imported-file copies; if cleanup cannot finish, it tells you to retry. Appearance preferences remain. Clearing the app does not delete original import files, separately saved backups or exports, Apple Health records, or copies already shared. Manage those copies in their respective apps or services. Deleting the app removes its local app container; offloading it retains app data. See Apple’s storage controls. Existing device or cloud backups may retain earlier copies.

Timers and notifications

If enabled, iOS may show workout and rest timer information in notifications or Live Activities on your Lock Screen. You can change these features in Settings → Timers & alerts and in iOS settings.

TestFlight and support

During TestFlight testing, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. These may include your name and email for an email invitation. Feedback and screenshots you submit are also shared. This is separate from the app’s local journal processing. Review Apple’s TestFlight privacy information and Apple’s privacy controls.

We use diagnostics and support messages to investigate problems and respond to you. We retain developer-controlled copies only as needed for that purpose, then delete them. Apple controls its own TestFlight records and retention under its policy.

Do not include private health records or full journal backups in a support message unless you intentionally choose to share them. Review screenshots before sending.

This website and external links

The policy and support website uses GitHub Pages. When you visit these pages, GitHub receives ordinary web requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or third-party embedded media. Following an external link brings you to that provider’s site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and updates

Health Coach is provided by Merle Klein. For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to the developer, email merlek.apps.support@gmail.com.

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Review an updated policy when new features change how information is handled.
